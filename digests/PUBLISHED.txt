Publishing
----------

GitHub Pages deploys the committed `build/` folder whenever changes are pushed
to `main`. Confirm that the `Deploy RV News` GitHub Actions workflow succeeds
before reporting an edition live.

The former Claude Artifact route was retired on 11 August 2026. Do not publish
or share a Claude Artifact for RV News.
