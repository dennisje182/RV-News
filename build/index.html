How this edition was put together

Coverage and judgement

331 articles were collected from 24 sources over 8 July 2026 to 9 August 2026. 100 were selected, 92 duplicate reports of the same story were merged into the entry that covered it best, and 139 were set aside as out of scope — travel features, campsite guides, prize draws, consumer round-ups and non-European trade news.

Article titles, dates, sources and links are taken directly from the publishers. English titles and summaries are translations, and the category, the competitor flag and every "why it matters" note are editorial judgement rather than anything the source said.

Built 9 August 2026 for Thetford product management. Colour, type and the sunburst come from the Thetford design system; contrast pairs were measured rather than eyeballed.

European RV manufacturers publish almost no machine-readable feeds, so new-vehicle coverage comes from the trade press rather than from OEM newsrooms. Feeds also expose only their most recent articles, so the collector pages backwards through each one to avoid missing the fast-moving titles.

To add or remove a source, edit sources.yaml in the project folder.
